Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Mahjongers (“we”, “us”) is operated by Move78 Hospitality, India. This policy explains what personal data we collect through mahjongers.com, why, and the choices you have. It is written to meet India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and, for visitors in the EU/UK, the GDPR.

What we collect

  • Newsletter sign-ups: your email address.
  • Contact and plan requests (the form at /join): your name, email, and — if you choose to give them — your phone number, a note about your mahjong work, and the plan you're interested in.
  • Usage data: privacy-friendly, aggregate analytics (pages viewed, referrer, country, device type) and standard security logs (IP address, browser) kept by our hosting provider.

We do not collect payment details on this website, and we don't sell your data.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your request and, if you become a client, to deliver our services (basis: your request / our contract with you).
  • To send our newsletter. Submitting the contact form also subscribes you; every email has a one-click unsubscribe link (basis: your consent).
  • To keep the site secure, stop spam and understand which pages are useful (basis: our legitimate interests).

Who we share it with

Only the service providers that run this site, each acting on our instructions:

  • Cloudflare — hosting, security and web analytics.
  • Beehiiv — newsletter delivery.
  • Resend — sends contact-form details to our team by email.
  • DataFast — website analytics.
  • Google Workspace — our team inbox.

Some of these providers process data outside India, including in the United States. We rely on their standard contractual protections for these transfers. We may also disclose data where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Newsletter data is kept until you unsubscribe. Contact-form details are kept for up to 24 months after our last conversation, or for the length of our contract plus any period the law requires (for example, for tax records). Security logs are kept for short periods by Cloudflare.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, update or delete your personal data, withdraw consent, or nominate someone to act for you. EU/UK visitors can also object to processing, ask for a copy in a portable format, and complain to their local data-protection authority. Email hi.mahjongers@gmail.com and we'll respond within 30 days.

Grievance Officer

If you have a complaint about how we handle your data, contact our Grievance Officer: Bhargavi, +91 78001 00178, hi.mahjongers@gmail.com. If you're not satisfied with our response, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.

Children

This site is for adults running mahjong businesses. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

Changes

We'll post any changes on this page and update the date above. For significant changes we'll tell newsletter subscribers by email.
