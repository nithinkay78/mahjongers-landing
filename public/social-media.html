A few questions

Who owns my accounts?

You do, always. We work as a team member on your accounts — you can remove our access at any time, and every post, photo and follower stays yours.

What do I need to send you?

Photos and short phone clips from your classes and games, plus your class dates and prices. A shared folder works fine. We handle the editing, writing and posting.

What if I don't like a post?

You approve the monthly calendar before anything goes out, and you can ask for changes at any point. Nothing posts that you haven't seen.

How soon can we start?

This week. After you sign up we book your intake call, and your first posts go out in your first week.

Can I cancel?

Plans have a 3-month minimum so there's time to see results. After that you're month to month — cancel with 30 days' notice.

Is ad spend included?

No. On Max (or the paid-ads add-on) we manage your ads, but the ad spend itself is paid by you directly to Meta or TikTok.
