A few questions

Do I own my website?

Yes. Your domain and all your content are yours. If you ever stop your care plan, we'll hand the site and files over to you or your next developer.

Can you guarantee ChatGPT will recommend me?

No one can — AI assistants choose their own answers. We do everything that makes a recommendation more likely (clear facts, structured data, consistent listings, reviews, open access for AI search) and show you each month what the main assistants say.

What's included in the care plan?

Hosting, security, backups, software updates, uptime monitoring and a set amount of small edits each month — new class dates, photo swaps, price changes.

I already have a domain — can I keep it?

Of course. We'll connect it for you. If you don't have one yet, we'll help you pick and register one in your name.

How do payments work?

Pro and Max take card payments through Stripe, straight into your own Stripe account — we never hold your money.

How long does it take?

Starter goes live in 15 days, Pro in 30 and Max in 60, counted from when we have your content and photos.
