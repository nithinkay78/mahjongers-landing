Staring at a blank caption box is the fastest way to stop posting. Here are 40 ideas you can use straight away, grouped by what they do for your business. Pick two or three a week and rotate.

Teach something (Reels and carousels)

These get saved and shared, which helps new people find you.

  1. The Charleston explained in under a minute.
  2. What a joker can and can't be used for.
  3. How to read this year's card — one section at a time.
  4. Three beginner-friendly hands to aim for.
  5. The difference between a pung, a kong and a quint.
  6. What to do when you're stuck with tiles you can't use.
  7. “Rules everyone gets wrong at their first game.”
  8. How to set up the wall, step by step, overhead shot.
  9. Mahjong etiquette: calling, exposing and table manners.
  10. American vs. Hong Kong vs. Riichi — a quick comparison carousel.

Show the table (photos, Stories and short clips)

These show what it actually feels like to spend an afternoon with you.

  1. Time-lapse of setting up for a game night.
  2. The snack spread (people love this one).
  3. Close-up of a beautiful tile set or rack.
  4. A student's very first Mahjong — the reaction says it all (with permission).
  5. Your playlist for game night.
  6. “A day in the life of a mahjong teacher.”
  7. Packing your bag for a class at a client's home.
  8. The view from your favourite venue.
  9. Before-and-after of a table styled for an event.
  10. Your own story: how you learned, and who taught you.

Build trust (social proof)

  1. A student testimonial as a simple quote graphic.
  2. “Where are they now?” — a beginner who now hosts their own table.
  3. Photos from a private party or corporate event you ran.
  4. Answers to your three most common DMs.
  5. A “meet the teacher” post with a friendly photo of you.
  6. Milestones: your 100th student, your 50th class.
  7. A thank-you post to a venue or partner.

Get people talking (engagement)

  1. Story poll: “What's harder — the Charleston or reading the card?”
  2. Quiz: “Can you spot the mistake in this hand?”
  3. “Caption this” with a funny table moment.
  4. Ask followers to share their favourite tile design.
  5. “Unpopular mahjong opinions” — invite debate.
  6. Story question box: “Ask me anything about mahjong.”

Ask for the booking (promo)

Don't skip these — they're what turns followers into students.

  1. Next month's class schedule as a carousel.
  2. “Only 3 seats left for Saturday.”
  3. A gift-card post ahead of holidays and birthdays.
  4. Private lessons and party packages, explained.
  5. Early-bird pricing for a new course or series.
  6. Bring-a-friend offer for your beginners' class.
  7. A countdown Story for a big event or tournament.

How to use this list

A simple weekly mix is one teaching post, one table post, and one promo post, with Stories in between. Every few weeks, swap in a trust or engagement post. Save this page and you'll have months of ideas ready.

Tip: film several ideas in one session. One game night can give you a set-up time-lapse, a snack shot, a student reaction and a teaching clip — four posts from one evening.
